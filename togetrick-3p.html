Path of Beginnings

Everyone needs to start somewhere.

Main challenge: Complete each of the following tasks in its own deal. Play each task in order. You may retry each task freely. Complete all tasks to complete this challenge.

  1. No player wins 2 tricks in a row.
  2. Each player wins at least 1 clubs (♣) card.
  3. The leftover card in the pool is 7 of Hearts (7♥).

Extra challenge: Instead of the main challenge, complete the above 3 tasks simultaneously in a single deal.
