Concepts

Individual voting

Some tasks are assigned to a single player. Paths have a variety of ways to assign the tasks, but a common method is individual voting. If a task is to be assigned by individual voting, follow these rules.

After distributing cards: Each player simultaneously extends their thumb up or down. Then the captain chooses one player with a thumb up; they get assigned the task.

  • The captain also extends their thumb up or down. If the captain shows a thumb up, they may choose themself.
  • If no player shows a thumb up, repeat this process until someone gets assigned the task.

Everyone needs to start somewhere.

Welcome to Togetrick! Path of Beginnings is your first step toward exploring the campaign. Follow the instructions for starting your campaign.

The Wilderness

The wilderness is not a path. It's a place for me to put various ideas I have that aren't yet integrated into paths.

Individual voting

Each task is assigned to one player by individual voting.

  • You must win at least 1 hearts (♥) card, and an equal number of diamonds (♦) cards.

Uses the other cards

Since the deck is only 33 cards, some cards are unused. These tasks make use of them. When these tasks say "red" honors, it's mostly to more easily distinguish them from the spades.

  • Take 1–8♠ plus 4 red honors. Shuffle them and distribute them equally to all players, then reveal them. (They are not part of the hand.) For each ♠, each player must win the trick numbered that rank. i.e. The 1st trick must be won by the player with 1♠; the 2nd trick must be won by the player with 2♠; etc.
  • Take 8 spades plus (3p) 7 / (4p) 8 honors. Shuffle them and distribute them equally to all players. (They are not part of the hand.) Each player may look at them, but do not show them to other players. You must win exactly the same number of tricks as you have spades.
  • Take 1–10♠ plus 2 red honors. Shuffle them and distribute them equally to all players, then reveal them. (They are not part of the hand.) You may not win any card of a rank represented by your ♠.
  • Some tasks in Ends of the Earth that use the number tokens can be adapted here. The most notable one I can remember: Take 1–3♠. The captain closes their eyes, then shuffle and reveal 2 of them. The captain must win tricks exactly equal to the sum, but they don't know this number. (Every other player knows.) Only the captain may communicate. (To prevent others from simply communicating the number somehow.) (There's a harder task with 1–4, but it seems difficult with only 8 tricks; the better extension might be 0–3.)
