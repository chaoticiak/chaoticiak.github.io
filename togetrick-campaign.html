Concepts

Individual voting

Some tasks are assigned to a single player. Paths have a variety of ways to assign the tasks, but a common method is individual voting. If a task is to be assigned by individual voting, follow these rules.

After distributing cards: Each player simultaneously extends their thumb up or down. Then the captain chooses one player with a thumb up; they get assigned the task.

  • The captain also extends their thumb up or down. If the captain shows a thumb up, they may choose themself.
  • If no player shows a thumb up, repeat this process until someone gets assigned the task.

As bots cannot vote, you make the decision on which bot gets assigned the task.

Helper cards

Some paths make use of the remaining cards that weren't used while preparing the 33-card deck. These cards are called helper cards, and they comprise of:

  • 1–10 of ♠ spades
  • Jack, Queen, and King of ♣ clubs, ♦ diamonds, and ♥ hearts
  • (Jokers are never needed. However, in some cases, they may help substituting for other cards. You may make use of them as you see fit.)

Not every helper card will be used each time. Refer to setup instructions to prepare the helper cards. Helper cards are usually face up in front of a player, although sometimes they are face down.

Helper cards never go into your hand. Make sure not to mix them with cards in your hand.

At the end of the deal, make sure to separate the helper cards from the rest of the deck.

Paths

Path of Beginnings

Everyone has to start somewhere.

Welcome to Togetrick! Path of Beginnings is your first step toward exploring the campaign. Follow the instructions for starting your campaign.

Main mission: Complete each of the following tasks, one each deal. You may retry tasks freely.

1st Every player must win at least 1 trick.
2nd Every card with rank 9 must win its trick.
3rd The unplayed card in the pool is 6♦ (6 of Diamonds).
Click once you've completed the main mission

Congratulations! In the future, this will unlock you several paths, but right now they are not yet ready.

You also have unlocked a secret extra mission for this path!

Extra mission: Play a single deal and complete all 3 tasks in the main mission simultaneously.

Click once you've completed the extra mission

Congratulations! At some point I will give you a reward for this mission, but not right now.

Path of Cooperation

Work together to tackle these tasks.

Main mission: Complete each of the following tasks, one each deal. You may retry tasks freely.

1st Every card played from the pool must win its trick.
2nd No trick may be led by a ♣ clubs card.
3rd Every trick must contain at least one card of rank 1–3.
4th In each trick, there must be at least one odd number rank and at least one even number rank. (Trumps are not number ranks and have no parity.)

Extra mission: Play two deals. For the first deal, choose any two of the above tasks, and complete them simultaneously. For the second deal, complete the other two tasks simultaneously. If you fail, you must start over (possibly with new choices).

Path of Courage

Muster the courage to take on these tasks while your fellow players support you.

Main mission: Complete each of the following tasks, one each deal. You may retry tasks freely.

Each task is to be assigned to a player by individual voting.

1st Win at least 5 tricks.
2nd Win at least 1 hearts (♥) card, and an equal number of diamonds (♦) cards.
3rd Win some trick using a card of rank 1.
4th Win all 9 other cards of the same suit as the leftover card in the pool. (The leftover card cannot be a trump.)

Extra mission: Play two deals. For the first deal, choose any two of the above tasks. Assign them with individual voting in either order (captain chooses), but the tasks must go to different players. Complete them simultaneously. For the second deal, assign and complete the other two tasks simultaneously in the same way. If you fail, you must start over (possibly with new choices).

Path of Recycle

The cards left out from the deck now get to play.

Main mission: Complete each of the following tasks, one each deal. You may retry tasks freely.

Setup: These tasks require the use of helper cards.

1st Helpers: K♣♦♥ (the Kings of Clubs, Diamonds, and Hearts). (4p) And 10♠ (10 of Spades).
Shuffle and distribute one card to each player, face-up.
Task: Each player may not win any card of the same suit as their helper.
2nd Helpers: 1–8♠ (1–8 of Spades), and any 4 red cards.
Shuffle and distribute equally to each player, face-up.
Task: Each player must win as many tricks as they have ♠ spades helpers.
3rd Helpers: 1–10♠ (1–10 of Spades).
Shuffle and distribute (3p) 3 cards / (4p) 2 cards to each player, discarding any left over. Each player chooses one helper they got and places it face-up in front of them.
Task: Each player must win (3p) all 3 cards / (4p) at least 2 cards of the same rank as their helper.
Click once you've completed the main mission

 

The Wilderness

The wilderness is not a path. It's a place for me to put various ideas I have that aren't yet integrated into paths.

Tasks that look better for a specific player count

These tasks are better for a specific player count.

  • (3p) Every player must win at least one ♥ hearts card. (4p) At 4p this becomes significantly harder, since the standard follow-suit-only tricks gets you 3 tricks but not 4.
  • (4p) No player may win two (or more) cards of the same rank. (3p) Disregard tricks containing trumps? Consider the pool as an extra player?
